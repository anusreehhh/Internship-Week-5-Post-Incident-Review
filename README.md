Week 5 – Post-Incident Review and Continuous Improvement Roadmap

Project Overview

This project focuses on conducting a post-incident review of the 2017 WannaCry ransomware attack that affected the United Kingdom's National Health Service (NHS).

The purpose is to understand the causes and impact of the cybersecurity incident, identify lessons learned, and develop a continuous improvement roadmap for strengthening cybersecurity in digital services.

Objectives

- Study a publicly known cybersecurity incident.
- Analyse its causes and impact on digital services.
- Understand the post-incident review process.
- Identify security weaknesses and lessons learned.
- Recommend practical improvements to prevent future incidents.

Case Study

Incident: WannaCry Ransomware Attack (2017)
Affected Organisation: United Kingdom National Health Service (NHS)

Main Areas Covered

- Incident overview and timeline
- Impact assessment
- Root-cause analysis
- Lessons learned
- Post-incident review process
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Continuous improvement roadmap
- Security performance indicators
